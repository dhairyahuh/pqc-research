## 1. Audit Principles and Statistical Grounding

In strict adherence to independent audit standards:
1. **Direct Derivation from Raw Execution:** Every metric is computed directly from raw trial logs generated by [`genuine_phase7_audit_harness.c`](file:///Users/dhairyajain/Desktop/pqc%20research/phase7_independent_claim_audit/genuine_phase7_audit_harness.c).
2. **Multi-Polynomial Reference Oracle:** Butterfly outputs are verified against the authentic reference of the *specific polynomial index* being transformed, eliminating the oracle collision identified in Phase 6.
3. **Strict Separation of H1 and H2:** Component-level verifier blindness (H1) and system-level silent data corruption (H2) are reported with independent numerators and confidence intervals.
4. **95% Wilson Score Intervals:** Confidence intervals are formulated using the two-sided Wilson score interval ($z = 1.95996$).
5. **No Model Pooling:** Fault models targeting different physical registers, logic paths, and mitigation layers are reported independently.
