## 1. Access Classification Taxonomy

To ensure full academic integrity and prevent unverified technical assumptions, every paper in this review is classified into one of three strict access levels:

1. **Level 1 — Full-Text Reviewed (Direct Verification):** The complete publisher version or authorized open-access preprint was retrieved, reviewed end-to-end (including methodology, derivations, experiments, and limitations), and verified.
2. **Level 2 — Partial / Preprint Reviewed (Tentative Verification):** An author preprint or extended technical report was examined, but the final copy-edited publisher version could not be inspected directly due to database paywalls. Findings are tentative pending final version confirmation.
3. **Level 3 — Unresolved Candidate (Metadata & Abstract Only):** Only the title, abstract, conference proceedings metadata, or citation snippets were accessible. **No methodology, data, or limitations are assumed or fabricated.** A legitimate retrieval route is documented for human follow-up.
